## Positioning (record it — this is the CRT comparison thesis)
**3Si does narrative + reputational monitoring.** 18 pages of online-content analysis; every threat factor (Surveillance / Protest / Work Interruption / Sabotage / Violence) rated **LOW** from online content alone; only Reputation raised to MEDIUM. **Nothing about proximity, evacuation, or physical threat to assets.**
**Fortress's claim is operational + proximity-anchored** — Bald Range distance *computed* (325 km, no established pathway → correctly declines to alarm), evacuation Orders near assets, corroborated ground truth, distances not asserted. **The four-lane comparison must NOT be "who writes a better weekly summary" — it must be a case where narrative monitoring is STRUCTURALLY BLIND:** a household evacuation Order 4.3 km from a school, or a fire-of-note distance call, is a class of finding 3Si's method cannot produce at all. Fortress loses on narrative polish today; it wins on the thing that matters when something is actually on fire.

## ADOPT from 3Si (Block A) — and what Fortress already has to populate each
1. **Deductions — ALREADY PRESENT, make it universal.** Fortress already ends sections with `DEDUCTIONS:` + a structured `IMPACT ANALYSIS` (worst-consequence / earliest-indicator / mitigation) that is *more* structured than 3Si's prose. Gap: not every item carries one, and the flash/awareness split sometimes drops the "what to expect next." Populate from: existing DEDUCTIONS + impact ladder; require one per main-tier item.
2. **Stable sections — PARTIAL.** Fortress has Operational / Regulatory / Social Sentiment. 3Si's taxonomy a reader learns to navigate: *Issues of Specific Concern · Issues of Concern to Industry · Connections to First Nations · Communities of Interest · Individuals of Note.* Adopt that spine. Populate from: existing signal categories (map Operational/Regulatory/Social → Specific/Industry), entity data for **Individuals of Note** (now unblocked by the entity-list cleanup — curated persons only), relationship graph for **Connections to First Nations**.
3. **Risk matrix + disclaimer — PARTIAL, and AHEAD on provenance.** Fortress has the 5-factor matrix. Add: **(a) a rating-definitions annex** (3Si Annex A: rating → probability → readiness posturing) so a named rating has a defined meaning; **(b) an explicit disclaimer** — "this is OSINT analysis, not presented as fact or a complete representation." Fortress's **provenance note** ("21 signals excluded for unresolvable provenance — review queue, not analyzed, not cited") is *stronger* than 3Si's disclaimer and should stay as the lead provenance statement, with the OSINT disclaimer added beside it.
4. **Analyst-comment device — ADOPT (the real gap).** 3Si pushes back on its own framing where a reader might over-infer (the Weaver item: *"ANALYST COMMENT: the hosts did not simply endorse Weaver's position…"*). Fortress has NO such device — and its absence is exactly what let the Lytton/Jasper over-inferences through (Defect 3). Populate from: the reliability-first / grounding discipline already in the codebase — a mandated "ANALYST COMMENT" wherever the report asserts a consequence a reader could over-read.

## FIX (Block B) — four defects in the current brief
1. **"Reliability Score: 0% | Sources: 0 verified | External Intel: NO"** on a report citing 11 sourced, linked signals — reads as Fortress declaring its own output unreliable. Computed by the reliability-first evidence gate (`runEvidenceGate` / `reliability-first.ts`; score logged at `generate-executive-report:2321/2336`). The edge function *already tries to strip the line* from the body (`index.ts:1079`) — someone flagged it — but it still reaches the PDF, so it is rendered by a separate footer/template path (not a literal in the searched code; assembled downstream). **RECOMMEND: REMOVE it.** The provenance note already carries reliability honestly; a 0% score that contradicts 11 cited signals is worse than nothing. (If kept, it must count cited signals with resolved `source_url` as verified — but removal is cleaner and the honest job is already done by the provenance note.)
2. **Action items assigned to roles that do not exist** — Intelligence Analyst / Chief Risk Officer / Sustainability Officer / Head of Regulatory Affairs / Legal-Compliance, with deadlines. Same defect as the digest phantom roles ([[WO-AGENT-FIRST-GOVERNANCE]] Defect 1), in the report layer (`generate-executive-report`). **Fix: either the client configures named owners, or actions are written as recommendations WITHOUT an owner and deadline.** Assigning 8/13/2026 to a role we invented is not credible.
3. **Two impact items with NO PECL nexus** — the Lytton First Nation fraud lawsuit and the Jasper tourism recovery, both given worst-case PECL consequences. This is the 665 problem in the report layer: **the brief tiers by `relevance_score` (`REL_MAIN=0.60`) and checks nexus ONLY for hazard-class signals** (the `hazard_pathway` cap that correctly sent Bald Range to awareness). **Non-hazard mis-attributions (a lawsuit, a tourism story) have no nexus gate — they ride relevance straight into IMPACT ANALYSIS.** Answer to the operator's question: **the brief does NOT filter on attribution quality; it consumes everything attributed to the client and tiers by relevance.** Fix: main-tier (impact / action / flash) requires `attribution_type='direct'` — the append-only attribution ledger already exists ([[WO-HONEST-ATTRIBUTION]] / `signal_client_attributions`); competitor/sector/none are excluded from impact, shown (if at all) as awareness "relevant to you, about X."
4. **KEEP + EMPHASISE** the **provenance note** and the **Bald Range treatment** ("~20,000 evacuated, no established pathway to PECL assets, nearest affected region 325 km from any corridor"). Proximity *computed, not asserted*, and it correctly declines to alarm. **These are the differentiators for the CRT comparison** — record them as such.
