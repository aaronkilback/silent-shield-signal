## What surfaced
Once the corroboration gate is honored (item 2), the Olynyk finding rests on **one** gate-passing domain
(63 captures, all from a single domain). Meanwhile the **Verified Public Presence** section requires **two or
more** independent domains by definition. So the report's only adverse *finding* is corroborated **more
thinly** than anything in the presence section — and **nothing in the report says so**. A reader sees "finding"
and assumes it is at least as well-supported as the presence items; it is not.

## The questions to answer (report, then decide)
1. **State it at the finding.** Should a finding that rests on one source say so on its face — e.g. "single
   source: this rests on one domain (N captures); it has not been independently corroborated" — so the reader
   weights it correctly?
2. **Sort.** Should single-source findings sort **below** multi-source findings within a section, so the
   better-corroborated items lead?
3. **Class.** Should a single-source item be a **finding** at all, or a **qualified** finding (a distinct
   state, like the synthesis `qualified`)? The verified-presence threshold is 2+ domains; a finding on 1
   domain is below even that bar. Is "finding" the right label, or should it be "unconfirmed / single-source"?
